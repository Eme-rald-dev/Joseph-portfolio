QualityLens

LLM Evaluation & Multimodal Data Annotation Dashboard

QualityLens is a dashboard concept that puts my AI training workflow on one screen. It tracks annotation volume, approval rate, review time and guideline adherence, and charts accuracy and quality over time.

It also shows how the work is done: labelling text, images, video and audio, and scoring an LLM response for helpfulness and accuracy, with a comments box for structured feedback. The design reflects three years of annotating data, evaluating model outputs and checking quality against detailed guidelines.

  • Annotation views for text, image, video and audio
  • Evaluation interface with rating options and feedback
  • Metrics for quality, speed and guideline adherence
  • Distribution of annotation work by data type

LLM QA Hub

LLM Response Evaluation & Quality Assurance Workbench

LLM QA Hub is a workbench concept for reviewing, rating and improving AI-generated responses. It shows a prompt next to two candidate answers, so a reviewer can compare them side by side before scoring.

Each response is scored against a rubric of accuracy, relevance, clarity, completeness and safety, and the scores roll up into an overall rating and an approve or flag decision. A QA summary and a performance trend help track review quality over time.

  • Side-by-side comparison of two responses to one prompt
  • Five-point rubric with a short note for each criterion
  • Overall rating with an approval decision
  • QA summary for responses reviewed, approvals and issues flagged
